# Dragon Fruit Trellis Hardware: Quote Checklist

A checklist for asking a supplier for a dragon fruit support system so the quotations can be compared.

## Decide first
1. Support style: single post with a top support, or continuous rows of posts joined by wire.
2. Plant spacing and row spacing on your plot (measure them, do not assume).
3. Post material and height above ground, plus depth in the ground.

## Send the supplier
Plot size, row length, post spacing, support style, the wire or top-support detail you want, fixing hardware, delivery location and quantity.

## Check the quotation
- Posts, wire and fixings listed as separate lines.
- The load the post and wire are meant to carry (a mature dragon fruit plant is heavy).
- Delivery and unit basis stated.
